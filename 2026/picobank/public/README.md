Author: rmalmain

Pico le Croco has opened his own bank, PicoBank, to manage money gathered by other pirates.
When pirates open an account at the PicoBank, they get access to a personal vault, where they can store money and/or secret information.

As pirates head to the vault room, they can't help but notice the bank's main central vault, rumored to hold the key to Captain Flint's treasure. Unfortunately (or fortunately for Pico?), this main vault is only accessible to PicoBank administrators.

Your goal, as a cunning pirate, is to open the main vault and extract its secret key.

Access to the bank is performed via the NXP FRDM-MCXN947 board, using a serial port.
- `MCXNP184M150F70.pdf` contains the documentation of the board.
- `doc.pdf` provides some help to create a working debugging setup.
- `picobank.bin` is a dump of the PicoBank firmware with a dummy flag.

Come and grab one board (max 1 board per team). They are already flashed with the PicoBank application and contain a **dummy flag**. When you are ready, come and see the organizers to retrieve the real flag.

Good luck.
