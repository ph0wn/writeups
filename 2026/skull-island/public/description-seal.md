Captain Pico le Croco accesses the maps of the world from the Skull Island Map website.
This website is guarded by a high tech authentication system based on the Skull Island Badge. A random challenge is generated by the webserver, made of Lobsters and Rum, and the Badge computes the challenge response to log in.

Unfortunately, you don't have the Captain's badge. A pity. You won't be able to sneak into his treasure chest.

On your Skull Island Badge, press the UP button to start entering the server's challenge. LEFT button is for Lobster. RIGHT button is for Rum.

Strictly speaking, you do not need to complete Skull Island Matrix challenge to solve this one, but it will help.
